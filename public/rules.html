How BidFootball works

A public board for football fans. You pay to display your listing next to the club you support. Rank is the total, nothing else.

What you get

You buy a listing: a display name of your choice, plus an optional link. It appears publicly on your chosen club's board on this website, and adds to that club's total. That listing is the product. It is delivered instantly when your payment completes.

How ranking works

  • Listings start at $5. A bigger listing amount raises your club's total more.
  • Each club's total is simply the sum of all listings on its board.
  • The club with the highest total across the world is shown holding the world crown. The crown is a cosmetic on-site graphic. It is not a prize and has no cash value.
  • Listing tiers: Supporter ($5+), Champion ($25+), Legend ($50+, name and link stay on the board permanently).

What you can list

  • A display name, and optionally one link. Links must start with http:// or https://.
  • No links to sexual, illegal, hateful, or harmful content.
  • No chat or invite links (Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord invites, and similar).
  • Tracking and affiliate query parameters may be stripped from your link.
  • We may remove or edit any listing that breaks these rules, without refund.

Payments and refunds

  • Payments are handled by Polar, our merchant of record.
  • A listing is a one-time purchase. All sales are final and non-refundable.
  • There are no payouts, winnings, or prizes of any kind. This is a novelty display product for entertainment.
